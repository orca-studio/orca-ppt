Insights

02

Insight #1

Trust is the onboarding.

Customers don't churn on day one. They churn because the first three emails feel like a stranger.

Insight #2

Power users dread upgrades.

The people we asked to love new features want fewer surprises, not bigger ones.

Insight #3

Support is product.

Half of "feature requests" in the inbox are discovery problems dressed as roadmap items.

Next quarter

06

Three experiments, one per insight.

Experiment 01

Write the first three emails by hand.

Every new account hears from the team that built what they bought.

Experiment 02

Ship changes with a preview week.

Power users see what's changing seven days early, and can choose to wait.

Experiment 03

Route discovery questions to product.

Inbox threads tagged "feature request" go to the team that owns that screen.
